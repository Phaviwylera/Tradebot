Hard rules

  • No orders. No wallet, key, signer or execution endpoint exists in this project.
  • Paper only. config.yaml sets mode: paper and allow_live_orders: false; the runner refuses anything else.
  • No model guessing. All six stages are deterministic code, not prompts to a language model.
  • No promised edge. A clean report means the workflow completed safely, not that the trade wins.
  • Manual approval. Nothing acts on a plan without a human.
Where this kind of setup usually fails
  • Unusual ≠ tradable. Most volume spikes are already priced in.
  • Headlines are claims. The News stage reads titles and timestamps, not article bodies.
  • Levels are noisy. Support and resistance are context, never a guarantee.
  • Fees decide marginal trades. The gate charges spread and a cost buffer before approval.
  • Synthetic trends are kind. Fixture approval rates say nothing about live performance.
  • Six stages are not independent. They share inputs; treat the pipeline as a checklist.
